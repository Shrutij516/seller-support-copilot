# Kestrel Market: Disputes

A dispute is opened when a buyer and seller cannot agree on the outcome of an order, or when
a buyer's payment provider initiates a chargeback. This policy covers how Kestrel Market
handles both cases.

## Buyer-seller disputes

Most order problems are resolved directly between buyer and seller through order messaging
and, if needed, the refund request process described in the Returns and Refunds policy. A
formal dispute is opened only when:

- A refund request has been open for more than 5 business days without seller response
  (this also triggers the auto-escalation described in the Returns and Refunds policy), or
- The buyer and seller have exchanged at least one message each but still disagree on the
  outcome, or
- Either party explicitly requests Kestrel Market mediation.

Once opened, both sides have **3 business days** to submit evidence: order details, messages,
photos, tracking information, or anything else relevant. A Kestrel Market mediator reviews
the evidence and issues a decision within **5 business days** of the evidence deadline. The
decision is binding within the platform: full refund to the buyer, partial refund, or no
refund with the order standing as delivered.

## Chargebacks (payment disputes)

A chargeback happens when a buyer disputes a charge directly with their bank or card issuer,
bypassing Kestrel Market entirely. When this happens:

1. Kestrel Market is notified by the payment processor and immediately places a hold on the
   disputed amount from the seller's payout.
2. The seller is notified and has **7 days** to submit evidence through the Seller Portal
   (proof of delivery, order communication, the listing as it appeared at time of sale).
3. Kestrel Market submits the strongest available evidence to the payment processor on the
   seller's behalf, but the payment processor's card network makes the final decision, not
   Kestrel Market.
4. If the chargeback is decided in the seller's favor, the held funds are released in the
   next payout cycle. If decided against the seller, the funds are permanently deducted and
   an additional **$15 chargeback fee** is applied to cover processing costs.

Chargebacks won by the buyer do not count against a seller's order defect rate in Account
Health if the seller submitted evidence within the 7-day window, since the outcome was
outside the seller's control once it reached the card network.

## Repeated disputes

A pattern of disputes from the same buyer across multiple sellers may indicate abuse of the
dispute process. Kestrel Market's Trust and Safety team monitors for this pattern separately
from any individual case and can restrict a buyer's account if abuse is confirmed. Sellers
who believe a specific buyer is abusing the dispute process can flag the order through the
Seller Portal for this review, though flagging does not pause or change the outcome of the
active dispute.

## What Kestrel Market does not mediate

Disputes about a policy itself (for example, a seller who disagrees with the 30-day refund
window) are not case-by-case mediation matters; policy feedback should go through the Seller
Portal's feedback channel instead. Disputes are also not opened for orders still within their
normal delivery window, since no problem has yet occurred to mediate.
