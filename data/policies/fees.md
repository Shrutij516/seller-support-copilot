# Kestrel Market: Fees

This policy explains every fee Kestrel Market charges sellers. All fees are deducted
automatically from a seller's payout; sellers never receive a separate invoice to pay
manually.

## Referral fee (per-item commission)

Kestrel Market charges a referral fee as a percentage of the item's sale price (not
including shipping charged to the buyer), varying by category:

| Category                    | Referral fee |
| --------------------------- | ------------ |
| Electronics                 | 8%           |
| Home and kitchen            | 12%          |
| Clothing and accessories    | 14%          |
| Handmade and craft supplies | 10%          |
| Books and media             | 15%          |
| Everything else (default)   | 12%          |

The referral fee has a minimum of $0.30 per item, so very low-priced items (under roughly
$2.50 at the default rate) are charged the $0.30 floor instead of the percentage.

## Fulfillment fee

Sellers who use Kestrel Market's fulfillment service (where Kestrel Market stores, packs,
and ships the item on the seller's behalf) pay a fulfillment fee based on package weight and
size tier, ranging from **$3.20** for a small standard package up to **$14.50** for an
oversized package. Sellers who handle their own shipping do not pay this fee.

## Storage fee (fulfillment service only)

Items stored in a Kestrel Market fulfillment center are charged a monthly storage fee per
cubic foot: **$0.75** per cubic foot from January through September, rising to **$2.40** per
cubic foot October through December to reflect peak-season warehouse demand. Inventory
stored longer than 365 days incurs an additional long-term storage surcharge of **$1.50** per
cubic foot on top of the standard rate.

## Subscription plans

Sellers choose one of two account plans:

- **Standard plan**: no monthly fee, $0.35 charged per item sold, in addition to the
  referral fee. Suited to sellers listing fewer than roughly 40 items per month.
- **Pro plan**: $34.99 per month flat fee, no per-item charge. Suited to higher-volume
  sellers. Sellers can switch plans once per calendar month, effective at the start of the
  next billing cycle.

## Refund-related fee adjustments

When an order is refunded, Kestrel Market returns the referral fee for that item to the
seller, since no completed sale occurred. The per-item fee (Standard plan) is not refunded,
as it covers listing and transaction processing costs already incurred. Fulfillment and
storage fees already charged are also not refunded, since those services were already
performed.

## Payout schedule

Payouts are issued every 14 days by default, covering all completed transactions from the
prior cycle, minus fees and any funds held for open disputes. Sellers in good Account Health
standing (see the Account Health policy) for at least 90 days can request a switch to a
7-day payout cycle through the Seller Portal.
