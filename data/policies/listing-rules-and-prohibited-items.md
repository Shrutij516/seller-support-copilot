# Kestrel Market: Listing Rules and Prohibited Items

Every listing on Kestrel Market represents the platform to a buyer, so listings are held to
a consistent set of rules regardless of category. This policy covers what a listing must
contain, and what can never be listed at all.

## Listing accuracy requirements

- **Title**: must describe the actual item for sale. Titles cannot include unrelated
  keywords added only to appear in more searches (keyword stuffing).
- **Photos**: at least one photo must be of the actual item being sold, not a stock or
  manufacturer photo, for any used, refurbished, or handmade item. New, factory-sealed items
  may use manufacturer photos.
- **Condition**: must be set accurately using Kestrel Market's condition scale (New, Like
  New, Good, Fair, For Parts). Misrepresenting condition to make an item appear newer than it
  is is treated as a misleading listing.
- **Price**: must reflect what a buyer actually pays before shipping and tax. Listing an
  artificially low price and adding mandatory "handling fees" at checkout is not allowed.

## Prohibited items

Some categories cannot be listed on Kestrel Market under any circumstances:

- Firearms, ammunition, and firearm conversion parts.
- Illegal drugs and drug paraphernalia, and any substance controlled under the seller's or
  buyer's local law.
- Live animals, except through Kestrel Market's separately regulated livestock program.
- Stolen goods, or items a seller has reason to believe are stolen.
- Recalled products that have not been remediated per the manufacturer's recall notice.
- Counterfeit goods, including items using a brand's name, logo, or packaging without
  authorization.
- Human remains or body parts, other than hair or teeth intended for craft use.
- Items that infringe someone else's copyright, trademark, or patent.

## Restricted items (allowed with conditions)

- **Alcohol**: only through Kestrel Market's licensed alcohol program, restricted to sellers
  who have submitted the required permits.
- **Prescription-adjacent medical devices**: allowed only from verified medical suppliers who
  have passed Kestrel Market's device-seller verification.
- **Replica or vintage weapons** (non-firing): allowed only if clearly described as
  non-functional and compliant with local replica laws.
- **Perishable food**: allowed only from sellers enrolled in the food-safety program, with
  appropriate cold-chain shipping.

## Enforcement

Listings that violate these rules are removed, and the seller is notified with the specific
reason. A first violation for a minor issue (like an inaccurate condition rating) typically
results in a warning and a request to fix the listing within 48 hours. Listing a prohibited
item results in immediate removal and a strike against the seller's account, regardless of
whether the item ever sold. Three strikes in a rolling 12-month period can lead to account
suspension pending review.

## Reporting a listing

Buyers and other sellers can report a listing they believe violates this policy from the
listing page itself. Reports are reviewed by Kestrel Market's Trust and Safety team, typically
within 24 hours for prohibited-item reports and 3 business days for accuracy disputes. The
seller is not notified of who filed a report.
