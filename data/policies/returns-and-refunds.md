# Kestrel Market: Returns and Refunds Policy

Kestrel Market wants buyers to feel confident purchasing from any seller on the platform,
and wants sellers to have clear, predictable rules for handling returns. This policy applies
to all standard listings unless a category-specific policy says otherwise.

## The 30-day refund window

A buyer can request a refund within **30 days** of the order's delivery date. The window
starts at delivery, not at purchase, since transit time varies by carrier and destination.
Requests submitted on day 31 or later are not eligible through the standard process and must
be escalated as a goodwill exception (see below).

An order becomes eligible for a refund request only after it is marked **delivered**. Orders
that are still pending, shipped, or in transit cannot have a refund requested against them;
the buyer should wait for delivery or contact the seller about a delayed shipment instead.

## What buyers can request

- **Full refund**: the item was not as described, arrived damaged, or never arrived despite
  showing as delivered.
- **Partial refund**: a minor issue (a missing accessory, small cosmetic damage) that doesn't
  justify a full return but still merits compensation.
- **Replacement**: the seller ships an identical item at no extra cost instead of refunding.

Only one open refund request is allowed per order at a time. If a request is resolved
(approved, denied, or withdrawn), a new request can be opened for the same order, but two
requests cannot be active simultaneously. This prevents duplicate cases from clogging a
seller's queue while the first request is still being reviewed.

## Seller responsibilities

Sellers must respond to a refund request within **3 business days**. If a seller does not
respond within 5 business days, the request auto-escalates to Kestrel Market's support team,
who will review the order and buyer messages and issue a decision on the seller's behalf.
Sellers who auto-escalate more than 3 times in a rolling 90-day period will see this reflected
in their Account Health score (see the Account Health policy).

Sellers do not need to accept a physical return before issuing a refund, but may request
photos of the item's condition or ask the buyer to ship it back at the seller's expense.
Requiring a buyer to pay for return shipping on an item that arrived damaged or misdescribed
is not permitted.

## What is not covered

- Buyer's remorse after the 30-day window has closed.
- Digital goods and gift cards, which are final sale unless defective.
- Custom or made-to-order items, unless they arrive damaged or significantly different from
  the listing description.
- Normal wear on items explicitly listed as "used" or "refurbished," where the wear was
  disclosed in the listing.

## Goodwill exceptions

Kestrel Market support can approve a refund outside the 30-day window in limited cases: a
verified shipping delay outside the buyer's control, a safety recall, or a documented carrier
error. Goodwill exceptions are funded by Kestrel Market, not deducted from the seller's
payout, and do not count against a seller's refund rate metric.

## Refund method and timing

Refunds are issued to the buyer's original payment method. Once a seller approves a refund,
funds are released within 2 business days. If Kestrel Market support issues the refund after
an escalation, the same 2-business-day timing applies, and the seller is notified of the
decision and the reason.
