# Kestrel Market: Shipping and Delivery SLAs

Buyers choose Kestrel Market because they trust orders will arrive when promised. This
policy defines the service-level agreements (SLAs) sellers must meet for handling and
shipping, and what happens when they're missed.

## Handling time

Handling time is the number of business days between an order being placed and the seller
handing it to a carrier. Kestrel Market recognizes three handling tiers, chosen by the seller
per listing based on their fulfillment capacity:

- **Same-day** (order placed before 2:00 PM local seller time ships that day)
- **Standard**: 1-2 business days
- **Extended**: 3-5 business days, available only for made-to-order or custom listings

A seller's actual handling time is measured against the tier they selected. Shipping later
than the selected tier on more than **5%** of orders in a rolling 30-day window triggers a
handling-time warning in Account Health.

## Delivery SLA by shipping speed

Once an order ships, Kestrel Market tracks delivery against the speed the buyer paid for:

| Shipping speed | Delivery SLA (business days from ship date) |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Standard       | 3-7 days                                    |
| Expedited      | 2-3 days                                    |
| Priority       | 1 day                                       |

These windows are estimates communicated to the buyer at checkout, generated from the
carrier's own service commitment plus a 1-day buffer for regional variation. A shipment
that is scanned "delivered" by the carrier but not received by the buyer is handled under
the Returns and Refunds policy, not this one.

## Late shipment protection

If a package has not moved past its expected delivery date, Kestrel Market's system flags
it as "late" automatically. The buyer is notified and offered the option to cancel for a
full refund if the seller does not provide a new estimated delivery date within 2 business
days of the flag. Sellers can proactively update tracking or contact the buyer to prevent
an automatic cancellation.

## Carrier and tracking requirements

Every shipment over $20 in value must include valid, scannable tracking from a Kestrel
Market-supported carrier. Orders shipped without tracking that a buyer disputes as
"not received" are resolved in the buyer's favor by default, since there is no way to verify
delivery. Sellers shipping internationally must use a carrier that provides tracking through
final delivery in the destination country, not just to the border or a regional hub.

## Lost and damaged in transit

If tracking shows no movement for **10 consecutive days** and the package has not reached
its destination, it is considered lost in transit. The seller may file a carrier claim, but
the buyer is made whole (refund or replacement) immediately and does not wait for the claim
to resolve. Items that arrive visibly damaged should be reported by the buyer within 48 hours
of delivery, with photos, to qualify for expedited replacement processing.

## Measuring seller performance

Kestrel Market calculates two shipping metrics per seller, visible on the Account Health
dashboard: **on-time handling rate** and **valid tracking rate**, both computed over a
trailing 30-day window. Falling below 90% on either metric for two consecutive weeks results
in a performance notice; sustained failure can lead to shipping-speed badges (like "Priority
eligible") being removed from a seller's listings until performance recovers.
